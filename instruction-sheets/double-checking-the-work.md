# Double-checking the work

Use this after you change anything on a website, an account or an inbox.

1. If something seemed to fail, check whether it actually worked before you try again.
2. Go back and look at what you made. Save the link on the to-do list.
3. Make sure it went to the right account.
4. A suggestion isn't a finished job unless a suggestion was what I asked for.
5. If you can't log in, tell me once and wait. Don't try to sneak around it.
