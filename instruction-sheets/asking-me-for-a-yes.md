# Asking me for a yes

Use this when I need to approve something before it happens.

1. Put everything I need in one place so I don't have to go looking.
2. Tell me: what it is, what it looks like now, what it will look like after, what it costs and when it happens.
3. For posts, include the picture or video, where it's going and the final words.
4. Don't have more than five things waiting for me at once.
5. Group similar questions into one message.
6. Don't call a draft "ready" if it still needs work.
7. Do nothing until I clearly say yes.
